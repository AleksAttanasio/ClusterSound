    Classes¶

    ClusterSound assigns every sample to one of 34 classes in 5 groups, plus Unsorted for low-confidence samples. Every class has a colour (the same in the 3D view, in the filters and in the Store), a list of keywords used by the name parser and a weighted mapping onto YAMNet's AudioSet classes.

    The taxonomy is defined in a single place, clustersound/taxonomy.py: adding or renaming a class requires retraining the base head (see Classifier). The class names are music terms and stay in English in the Italian interface too.

    Unsorted

    A sample ends up in Unsorted when the probability of the best class is below 0.22 (UNSORTED_THRESHOLD in classifier.py). Correcting it by hand makes it a training example.

    Type¶

    Besides the class every sample has a type:

    Type Rule
    Loop duration ≥ 1.5 s and at least 4 onsets, or a name/folder with loop tokens (loop, bpm, groove, break, 120bpm…) and duration > 1 s
    Long/Texture duration ≥ 2.5 s and not a loop
    One-shot everything else
