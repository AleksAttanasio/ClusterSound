# Manual

How to use every section of the app. The panel on the left groups the sections into **Sample** (Library, Store,
Recorder) and **Create** (Sequencer, Drum Rack, Compose), with **Settings** at the bottom.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Interface](interface.md)** — navigation, settings, flow between the sections.
- **[Library](library.md)** — the 3D cloud, sample details, filters, corrections.
- **[Store](store.md)** — free samples and packs from Freesound, Openverse, Internet Archive, ccMixter.
- **[Recorder](recorder.md)** — records the audio output, slices, separates stems, restores with AI.
- **[Sequencer](sequencer.md)** — tries samples on 191 grooves in 47 genres and exports for Ableton.
- **[Drum Rack](drum-rack.md)** — 8×8 kits for Launchpad and kits for the Roland SPD-SX.
- **[Compose](compose.md)** — chord progressions with suggestions and variations.
- **[Melody](melody.md)** — melodies, bass lines, arpeggios and riffs over the chords.
- **[Max for Live](max-for-live.md)** — imports everything into Ableton Live without dragging files.

</div>
