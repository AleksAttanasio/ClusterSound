    Recorder¶

    Records what the computer sends to one or more audio outputs, then cuts samples out of the recording: they go into the library and are classified like the others. It also opens any audio file or library sample.

    Recording¶

    • Outputs: speakers, headphones, audio interfaces, AirPlay… plus All outputs (mix) (on Windows Default output). Several outputs start together, each with its own file and meter. Refresh the list reads the devices again.
    • Format (Settings › Recorder): WAV 16/24 bit or 32 float, AIFF, FLAC 16/24, CAF, MP3 128–320 kbps, OGG Vorbis, Opus (48 kHz), M4A/AAC (macOS only). Device rate or 44.1/48/96 kHz; stereo, mono or all channels (uncompressed formats).
    • Folder: ~/Music/ClusterSound Recordings/<date time> - <output>.<ext> with a .source.txt of provenance. The recording goes on while you switch section.

    Opening audio¶

    Open file… (or drag from the Finder onto the waveform) opens any audio file; From the library… opens samples and loops browsing folders and packs or searching by name.

    Editor and regions¶

    • When you stop, the recording opens in the editor. Drag on the waveform to create a region, the edges resize it, click to listen to it.
    • Slice on transients creates one region per hit in the visible part; Sensitivity sets how many hits it finds.
    • Wheel: zoom · Shift+wheel: scroll · the strip below moves the view · Space play/stop · ↑ ↓ previous/next region · Del delete.

    Exporting¶

    • Export selected: 24-bit WAV in ~/Music/ClusterSound Samples/, with fades (1 ms in, 10 ms out) and optional normalisation, at most 30 s each.
    • To the Drum Rack: loads the regions on the free pads from the selected pad on, in note order, and opens the Drum Rack.
    • Send to Live: a new audio track in Ableton with the region (or all the audio), in the first empty slot of the selected scene. It needs the ClusterSound device with Receive on.

    Extract stems¶

    Separates the recording or the region into Drums, Bass, Guitar, Piano, Vocals (those you tick) plus always Other with the rest, so the sum gives back the original. One 24-bit WAV per stem, which can be opened in the editor. It runs locally with Demucs (stems extra, 55 MB model downloaded on first use). Details: Demucs.

    Enhance¶

    Purple ✨ button, also on every region: AI restoration that rebuilds the cut high frequencies and cleans up the artefacts of compression (MP3, streaming). The result is a new … (enhanced).wav file; the original stays as it is. It runs locally with Apollo (enhance extra, 66 MB). Details: Apollo.

    Legal note

    Nothing is decrypted or bypassed: apps that protect their stream record silence. Recordings are for personal use (private copy); to publish a track that contains a sample you need the rights to the original, or a sample with a free licence. There is no number of seconds below which a sample is free.

    Windows

    Every output is recorded with WASAPI loopback: there is no mix of all the outputs, ClusterSound's own audio also ends up in the recording and the M4A format is not available. No permission to grant.
