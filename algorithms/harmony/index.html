    Harmony and chords¶

    Code: chords/library.py, theory.py, harmony.py, variations.py, perform.py. Chords are not generated from abstract rules: they are the MIDI clips of the chord pack, indexed and then sorted, transposed and varied. This way every chord has a voicing played by a musician.

    Indexing the pack¶

    • Key from the NN - X Major - Y Minor folders; the mode from the presence of "minor" in the subfolders; the category (triads, extended, borrowed, progressions…) from keywords of the folders.
    • Segmentation: notes with onsets within 1/32 form a chord (resampled to 480 PPQ); every chord lasts until the next one and the end of the file is rounded to the bar. A file with several segments is a progression; the degrees in brackets in the name ((I-V-vi-IV)) are read if they are all valid.
    • Root: from the name of the chord, otherwise from the degree, otherwise estimated (below).
    • Missing keys: served by the nearest key of the pack with the same category, transposing by −6…+5 semitones (on a tie, downwards). The results are marked transposed.
    • Statistics: for every progression the bigrams of degrees are counted (consecutive pairs, repetitions excluded; the last → first return counts 0.5 because progressions loop).

    Theory (theory.py)¶

    Quality from the intervals above the root: with a major third → augmented (augmented 5th without a perfect one), dominant (minor 7th without a major one) or major; with a minor third → diminished (diminished 5th without a perfect one) or minor; otherwise sus.

    Estimated root: for every pitch class r

    \[ \text{score}(r) = 2[\text{3rd}] + 1.5[\text{5th}] + 1[\text{dim. 5th with min. 3rd}] + 0.5[\text{7th}] + 0.75[r = \text{bass}] \]

    Harmonic function: a chord is diatonic if the root and the triad core (root, 3rd, 5th) are in the scale (in minor the leading tone is allowed too). Diatonic chords have a Tonic, Subdominant or Dominant function by degree; all the others are C (colour / modal borrowing). These are the four colours of the browser.

    Next chord¶

    For every candidate b after the chord a, transition(a, b) adds up:

    Term Value
    Function flow T/S/D/C table: e.g. T→S 1.0 · S→D 1.0 · D→T 1.0 · T→D 0.75 · D→S 0.3
    Cadence (the first that matches) V→I +0.6 · ii→V +0.45 · plagal +0.35 · deceptive +0.35 · bVI→bVII +0.35 · mixolydian bVII→I +0.35 · Neapolitan +0.3 · leading tone→tonic +0.3 …
    Bass motion fourth up (fifth down) +0.35 · fifth up +0.15 · stepwise +0.15 · tritone −0.3 · same root −0.3 (different colour) or −0.7
    Common notes +0.08 per note, up to 3
    Voice leading \(\max(0,\;0.3 - 0.03\,d)\), with d = mean distance in semitones
    Frequency in the pack \(+0.6 \cdot \text{bigram}(a,b) / \max\)

    The voice leading distance between two voicings is the mean of two means: every note of b to the nearest note of a, and vice versa.

    Then suggest:

    • a −0.8 penalty for the same chord of the library and −0.15 for an A-B-A ping-pong;
    • if there is already a chord n after it, \(0.6 \cdot \text{transition}(b, n)\) is added: the suggestion also has to lead well;
    • without a previous chord, starting score: T 1.0 (+0.4 if it is the I) · S 0.55 · D 0.35 · C 0.2;
    • the candidates are grouped by (root, minor, diminished): the best voicing wins, the others become the colour variants under the proposal;
    • the scores are rescaled between 0.35 and 1 for the bar; the reasons shown are the labels of the terms that fired.

    With an unlocked key the candidates include the nearby keys: dominant, subdominant, relative and parallel.

    Variations¶

    All deterministic given the seed (random.Random(seed)).

    Fitting a voicing (_fit): tries inversions −2…+2 × octaves −1…+1 (notes between 24 and 100) and picks the one that minimises \(d(\text{previous}) + 0.15\,|\text{centre} - \text{reference centre}|/3\).

    Of a chord:

    • voicing: 1st and 2nd inversion, inverted downwards, drop-2, open (2nd voice an octave up), close, doubled bass; at the top the one with minimal voice leading from the previous chord;
    • colour: same root and same mode from the pack, preferring a different number of notes;
    • substitution: the first 2 of suggest in context (chord before and after) plus 2 random ones among the others.

    Of the progression (vary_progression), with How much to vary \(a \in [0.05, 1]\):

    Kind Strategy
    reharmonisation replaces \(\operatorname{round}(a \cdot \text{inner})\) chords (first and last stay); every choice takes rank \(\lfloor u^2 \cdot 5\rfloor\) among the 5 best suggestions, with u uniform: it favours the best without always being the same
    harmonic rhythm eighth-note anticipation, long-short (1.5 + 0.5), split of a chord ≥ 1 bar with the best suggestion in the second half
    voice leading chain of _fit for minimal motion
    richer / sparser same root with more or fewer notes (sparser prefers triads)
    ending typical cadence of the mode on the last 2 chords (IV→V, bVI→bVII, IV→iv, ii→V…)

    The proposals are deduplicated by signature; up to 4× the number requested are generated.

    Playing (perform.py)¶

    From chords to notes, at 480 PPQ; the same function produces preview, MIDI and clips for Live.

    • Hits: the rhythm grid repeats over the absolute timeline; a length of 0 means "until the next hit". A chord that starts off the grid gets a hit at its start.
    • Swing: odd sixteenths delayed by \((\text{swing}/50 - 1)\cdot 120\) ticks.
    • Strum: guitar direction (down on the beats, up in between), alternate, always up/down; total spread limited to half the length; linear, accelerating \(1-(1-x)^2\) or decelerating \(x^2\) curve; velocity tilt and softer upstrokes; upstrokes can be limited to the top notes.
    • Arpeggio: up, down, up-down, random over 1–3 octaves; off-beat notes at 0.86 of the velocity.
    • Dynamics: crescendo \(0.72 + 0.42f\), diminuendo \(1.14 - 0.42f\), bass accent ×1.12; accents on the beats.
    • Deterministic humanize (humanize seed XOR CRC32 of the chord): timing shifts a whole hit together, with a drift that mixes white noise and cosine-interpolated "value" noise (period 2 beats); plus per-note spread, length ±% and velocity ±. Finally overlaps on the same note are shortened and notes under 12 ticks are dropped.
